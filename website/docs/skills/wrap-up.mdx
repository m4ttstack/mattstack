# wrap-up

`mattstack:wrap-up` ends a turn that has open decisions with a form instead of a wall of prose.

## What you ask for

Invoke it when wrapping up a session, checking in before continuing, or when you ask what Claude needs from you, which decisions are open or what the next steps are. It also presents a pipeline gate's decision as a form.

## What you get

One optional sentence of context, then a form, then Claude stops and waits for your answers before doing more work. The form is Claude Code's structured-question tool, with one question per open item in three buckets (an empty bucket is left out):

| Bucket | The question is | Options |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Important details | A confirmation or pick among facts that still matter | Concrete values |
| Decisions | A choice only you can make | The real alternatives, recommended first and labelled `(Recommended)` |
| Next steps | Whether or in what order to do remaining work | Do now, later or skip |

Questions can be single or multiple choice. If the tool caps how many fit in one call, the first batch is asked, and the rest follow after you answer.
