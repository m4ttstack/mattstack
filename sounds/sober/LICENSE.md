# Sober Ringtones

The four notification tones in this folder are from Sober Ringtones by
wize.io, https://sober-ringtones.wize.io (source:
https://github.com/frabonomi/sober-ringtones), renamed from
`<n>-notification.mp3` to `<n>.mp3` and otherwise unmodified.
